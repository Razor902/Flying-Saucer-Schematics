TRIP–01 · 86 LOCKOUT Containment drift

TRIP–02 · 86 LOCKOUT Converter overspeed

TRIGGER
TC–20 above 115% speed; alarm at 110%.

  1. Close the dump contactor; unload into DL–01 for dynamic braking.
  2. Trip mechanical isolation.
  3. Block reclose until speed decays.

MANUAL RESET
Speed below 5% and DL–01 thermally healthy.
