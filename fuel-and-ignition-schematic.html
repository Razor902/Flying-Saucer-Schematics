The coil is an electrically powered ignition and plasma-control load—not a second fuel and not an energy multiplier.

SIZING LOGIC

Fuel flow = demanded thermal input ÷ fuel heating value. Demanded thermal input must include converter efficiency and every auxiliary load, including pumps, cooling and the RF ignition drive. The coil changes ignition quality; it does not reduce the energy balance to zero.

Start and stop choreography

Sequencing is what keeps a plausible block diagram from becoming an uncontrolled fuel-and-spark arrangement.

Normal start

  1. 01
    Prove the boundaryChamber closed; cooling, exhaust and sensors healthy.
  2. 02
    Purge firstMove several validated chamber volumes of inert gas; prove flow.
  3. 03
    Arm ignitionEnergize RF drive at limited power; verify isolation and field current.
  4. 04
    Admit reactantsOpen oxidizer and fuel through independent metering within the validated envelope.
  5. 05
    Prove flameRequire flame / plasma confirmation inside a short trial window or trip.
  6. 06
    Ramp under controlIncrease thermal load while pressure, mixture and cooling remain in bounds.

Immediate trip

  1. 01
    Fuel valves fail closedRemove chemical energy at the source; do not depend on software alone.
  2. 02
    Block RF driveOpen the drive contactor and discharge stored electrical energy safely.
  3. 03
    Keep cooling aliveCarry residual heat out unless a leak condition requires isolation.
  4. 04
    Inert and ventPurge residual reactants through the designed treatment path.
  5. 05
    Latch lockoutAn 86-style lockout prevents automatic restart.
  6. 06
    Inspect before resetReset only after pressure, temperature, gas and insulation checks pass.

Hard interlocks

Any one of these conditions removes fuel and ignition. Safety energy always has a known destination.

PSHH-01

High chamber pressure

Close fuel, block ignition, maintain qualified relief and cooling paths.

FSL-02

Loss of cooling flow

Stop heat input before stored vessel heat exceeds the cooling margin.

BMS-03

Flame not proven

End the trial-for-ignition window, isolate fuel, then complete a full purge.

64G / 50G

RF ground fault

Open the RF source, discharge the resonant network and inhibit restart.
