What this is. A vendor-neutral, block-level reference schematic — the architecture view investors and partners look at first: cores, cache hierarchy, fabric, memory, I/O, platform. Real vendor silicon (Intel, AMD, Apple, Qualcomm) is proprietary down to the transistor; nobody publishes true gate-level schematics. This is the clean-room starting point a new company designs from.

What comes next on the road to real silicon. RTL in Verilog/VHDL → functional simulation → synthesis to a process node → place & route → tapeout at a foundry (TSMC, Intel Foundry, Samsung). A first test chip is routinely a 7-to-9-figure undertaking — which is exactly why the pitch starts with architecture, team, and the system around the chip.

The honest edge here. Founders who understand silicon and the hardened, tethered systems around it are rare. An ecosystem of tungsten-armored nodes with AI tethers is a story no commodity chip company tells. That is the differentiator to lead with in the tank.
