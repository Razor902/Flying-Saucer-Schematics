      Physics boundary: Alcubierre’s metric is a mathematical spacetime geometry, not a demonstrated propulsion device. No known method can create, contain, steer, or safely collapse the required negative-energy distribution. WRP-01 and WRP-02 are therefore placeholders for unresolved physics; the switching, segmentation, fault containment, and interlocks around them use ordinary engineering practice.

      Non-negotiable design rules

      These decisions keep one failure from becoming a craft-wide loss.

      R-01
      No single energized hull return

      Use controlled bonding and monitored return conductors; isolate noisy pulse loads.

      R-02
      Essential bus survives source loss

      Independent battery supports flight control, navigation, comms, and atmosphere.

      R-03
      Warp branch is normally open

      Physically separated contactors and discharge path; no software-only inhibit.

      R-04
      Heat is a first-class load

      Every power conversion block closes its thermal path before energization.

      R-05
      Graceful degradation

      Sector faults isolate locally; craft retains attitude, comms, and life support.
